BubbleUp

The app to help you navigate safe social gathering during the pandemic.

We're still in development – sign up, and we'll let you know as soon as we launch!

One of the most commonly recommended ways to socialize while minimizing risk during the pandemic is by creating a "bubble". Eventually, however, people in the bubble will leave the bubble. Work, doctor's appointments, family trips, and other exceptions are inevitable, but keeping your bubble up to date with everyone's activities is difficult when communication is spread across phone calls, group chats, personal texts, long chain emails, and more.

If only there was one place to act as a source of truth, where you could gather and manage the information your bubble needs to operate safely...

Meet BubbleUp.

Create a bubble, add your friends, establish your bubble's guidelines and use our quick check-in to share recent activity information. Next time you want to hang out, BubbleUp to see:

  • the last time your friends socialized outside the bubble
  • if they were indoors, wearing a mask, and socially distant
  • if they used public transport
  • their recent COVID-19 test dates and results
  • and more, based on your bubble's shared guidelines and what your friends report!

Rather than pretending these exceptions will never happen, BubbleUp helps you include these variables as a realistic part of your planning. Because we all deserve to feel empowered when making decisions about our own safety.

Your information is only visible to the people in your bubble.

We will never track or ask your location, never gather any data using background services, and never sell your information. All activity information is self-reported by you, and never shared outside your bubble. Every check-in question is optional, and the only "personal" information we require is an email address & username. Share confidently with your bubble, knowing nobody else will ever see it.

Simple, social, safe.
