BubbleUp

The app to help you navigate safe social gathering during the pandemic.

Many health authorities recommend creating a "bubble" to socialize while minimizing risk – but eventually, people in the bubble will interact with people outside the bubble. Work, doctor's appointments, family trips, and other exceptions are inevitable, but keeping your bubble up to date with everyone's activities requires a lot of communication and coordination...

Meet BubbleUp.

Create a bubble, add your friends, and use our quick check-in to share information about recent activity. Next time you want to get together, BubbleUp to see when your friends last socialized outside the bubble, if they used public transport, their recent COVID-19 test dates and results, and more. Rather than pretending these exceptions will never happen, BubbleUp helps you include these variables as a realistic part of your planning. Because we all deserve to feel empowered when making decisions about our own safety.

Your information is only visible to the people in your bubble.

We will never track or ask your location, never gather any data using background services, and never sell your information. This is a promise we take extremely seriously. All activity information in BubbleUp is self-reported by you, with as much or little detail as you choose to give, and never shared outside your bubble. Every check-in question is optional, and the only "personal" information we require is an email address & username. BubbleUp exists to organize information, not gather it. We want to encourage honest and complete sharing of information within your bubble, for everyone's benefit.

BubbleUp: simple, social, safe.
