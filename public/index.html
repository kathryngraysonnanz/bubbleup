Are you part of a Covid-19 social bubble?

One of the most commonly recommended ways to socialize while minimizing risk during the pandemic is by creating a "bubble". Eventually, however, people in the bubble will leave the bubble. Work, doctor's appointments, childcare arrangements, family trips, and other exceptions are inevitable, but keeping your bubble up to date with everyone's activities is hard when communication is spread across phone calls, group chats, personal texts, long chain emails, and more.

If only there was a hub where you could gather and manage all the information your bubble needs to operate safely...

Meet BubbleUp: simple, social, safe.

Create a bubble, add your friends, establish your bubble's guidelines and use our quick check-in to share recent activity information. Next time you want to meet up, BubbleUp to see:

  • the last time your friends socialized outside the bubble
  • if they were indoors, wearing a mask, and socially distant
  • if they used public transport
  • their recent COVID-19 test dates and results
  • and more, based on your bubble's shared guidelines and what your friends report!

Rather than pretending these exceptions will never happen, BubbleUp helps you include these variables as a realistic part of your planning. Because we all deserve to feel empowered when making decisions about our own safety.

Your activity is only visible to the people in your bubble.

We will never track or ask your location, never gather any data using background services, and never sell any data. All activity information is self-reported by you, and never shared outside your bubble. Every check-in question is optional, and the only "personal" information we require is an email address & username. Share confidently within your bubble.
